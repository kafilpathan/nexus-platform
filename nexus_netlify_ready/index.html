The mission

Make difficult problems easier to tackle—together.

NEXUS aims to create a place where problems can meet many perspectives, AI-assisted research, and a path toward testing what might work.

◎

Bring problems into the open

Give people a clear way to describe a challenge, share context, and explain what a useful solution would look like.

⌘

Connect human and AI thinking

Use AI to organize questions and explore approaches, while people contribute experience, creativity, and critical judgment.

↗

Move from ideas to evidence

Compare proposals, surface weaknesses, and record tests so promising solutions can earn trust instead of relying on votes alone.

Our approach

We don't need to know every answer today. We need a better way to ask, explore, collaborate, and test.

NEXUS is at the beginning. The first version will be small and practical, then grow based on what real people find useful.

Solution lab

Ideas should earn trust.

As NEXUS develops, each problem can move through clear stages instead of calling an idea “solved” too early.

○

Open

The challenge has been recorded and needs clearer questions, context, or contributors.

⌕

Under research

People are gathering evidence, comparing approaches, and checking assumptions.

✓

Tested / resolved

A solution should reach this stage only when there is evidence or a documented test supporting the result.

This is a proposed workflow, not a live public problem database yet.

Privacy & trust

About NEXUS

A project at the beginning of its journey.

NEXUS is an early-stage vision for collective problem-solving powered by human curiosity and AI tools. We want to make it easier for people to submit meaningful problems, contribute ideas, challenge assumptions, and work toward tested solutions.

We're starting small, learning as we build, and inviting thoughtful feedback. We are not claiming a finished platform, a large team, or proven scientific breakthroughs.
